About RM Robiur

My name is Robiur, everybody calls me 'Roby'. I'm from India. I was born on 13 June 1993. From my childhood, I was a bit different, as told by my Mummy. Unfortunately, my father died in 2003, when I was only 10 years old.

Being poor, I studied at "Mulnivasi Mission" school for six years. In 2010, I was selected as a teacher in the same school. Now life goes on with learning and teaching. I'm connected with a Social Reforming Organization.

Travelling, conferences, cultural ceremonies, writing, and reading are my hobbies. I want to serve my society, especially the poor and backward sections. Hope my goal will be successful by the grace of my Creator.

📜 Poetry

ভোরের পাখি ডাকার আগে উঠব আমি জেগে

ভোরের পাখি ডাকার আগে উঠব আমি জেগে,
মুয়াজ্জিনের মধুর আজান বাজে হৃদয় বাগে।
ফজর হল পূর্ব দিকে শুভ্র কিরণ দুয়ার ফাঁকে,
শয্যা ছেড়ে রবের ডাকে, আলোয় ভরা ঈমান জাগে।
